Beatles Info Site

The Beatles were arguably the most important and influential music group of the 20th century and possibly of all time. Their contributions to music performance and recording affect most of all modern music, and their contributions will likely continue to affect the way music is played and recorded for many years to come.

This site is an unofficial informational site documenting various facts about members, instruments, songs, and techniques of The Beatles.

I hope that you enjoy learning about The Beatles from this site whether you are a brand-new fan or have been listening to them for many years.

Summary of The Beatles

The Beatles were formed in Liverpool, England in 1960 by 3 musicians: John Lennon (Vocalist, Rythmn Guitarist, Pianist), Paul McCartney (Vocalist, Bassist, Pianist), and George Harrison (Lead Guitarist, Vocalist). All 3 were members of another band known as The Quarreymen. Later on, drummer Pete Best joined The Beatles in August of that year. He was replaced by drummer Ringo Starr in August 1962. This lineup of John, Paul, Ringo, and George came to be known as The Fab Four, and remained constant until The Beatles eventually broke up in April of 1970.

Throughout their career, The Beatles recorded 12 studio albums. These albums can be roughly divided into 3 eras. These eras are called different names by different people, but I would call them:

  1. The Early Beatles Era
  2. The Studio Experimentation Era
  3. The Later Beatles Era

While The Beatles did innovate musically during the early beatles era, they are most remembered for their contributions later on in the studio experimentation era and the later beatles era. Most notably their work off of Sgt. Pepper's Lonely Heart's Club Band, The Beatles (often referred to as The White Album due to its lack of title), and Abbey Road.
